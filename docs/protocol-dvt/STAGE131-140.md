# Batch Steps 131–140 — Vary, Spec and Strong identity coverage

## Step 131 — Runtime wiring audit
A build-only gap was found: constants for the Step111–130 Faint additions existed, but statusFromSkillId still returned NONE for them. The resolver is now wired so Taunt and the extended Faint identities can actually be applied at runtime.

## Step 132 — Vary mapping
Direct VarySkill mappings are now represented: Boundary, IceWall, Limpid, Enlarge, Avoid, Mirror, SoulMirror, God, SameHeart, Inspire, FireAmulet, TransferAttack, Invisible, GoldShield, DolphinGuard, ImmunityShield, IceCrystals and DragonTransfer.

## Step 133 — Vary group
All those identities route to client EFightStatusKind.Vary / group 2 and therefore share the proven Vary replacement/dispel lifecycle.

## Step 134 — Spec mapping
20006 Protect, 20008 Spell and 14056 MagicProtect are modeled from SpecSkill.

## Step 135 — Spec group
Protect, Spell and MagicProtect route to EFightStatusKind.Spec / group 4.

## Step 136 — Strong mapping
BrightMirror, WarStep, WildFightSoul, AngryMind, MagicalShield, CrazySoul and PitySoldier are modeled from StrongSkill.

## Step 137 — Strong group
Those Strong identities route to EFightStatusKind.Strong / group 5 and use the existing Strong clear list.

## Step 138 — Lua precedence
Overlapping skill IDs are resolved in the same first-match order as the client mapping function. A single client status group remains one active identity at a time.

## Step 139 — Formula boundary
This batch models identity, group, duration and dispel only. Reflect, dodge, stat modifiers, shields, protection transfer and immunity formulas are not inferred from animation/status names.

## Step 140 — Build gate
Run ./gradlew test build. After this gate, audit concrete combat consumers for the newly modeled Vary/Spec/Strong identities and implement only source-proven effects.
